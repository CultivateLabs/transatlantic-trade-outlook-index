# Published calculation files

`2026-09-28-current-state.csv` contains the exact inputs behind the four quantitative readings in the 28 September 2026 edition. Dollar totals are stored at full precision; displayed percentages are rounded only after aggregation.

The source is the U.S. Census Bureau’s [monthly merchandise data products](https://www.census.gov/foreign-trade/data/dataproducts.html). Tariff rates use imports for consumption, customs value and calculated duties. Trade volume uses imports for consumption plus total exports. The EU means its 27 members, entered individually; the other covered partners are the United Kingdom and Switzerland. Non-monetary gold under HTS/Schedule B 710811, 710812 and 710813 is removed from the Swiss rate and both legs of the trade-volume calculation.

The July 2026 official source archives are [imports](https://www.census.gov/trade/downloads/2026/Merch/im_m/IMDB2607.ZIP) and [exports](https://www.census.gov/trade/downloads/2026/Merch/ex_m/EXDB2607.ZIP). Earlier months follow the same Census URL convention and are identified by the observation periods in the CSV.
